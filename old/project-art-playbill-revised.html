Theatre promotion · Print design

ART — Playbill

Promotional print design for a local theatre production, bringing the play’s identity to life through a considered visual presentation.

Discipline
Print design
Focus
Typography, layout, promotional design
Project
05 / 08
Discuss a project ↗

Overview.

Created for ART by Yasmina Reza at Town & Gown Players, this project explores how print design can support a theatrical production and give audiences a first impression of the show. The work focuses on presenting the production clearly while using typography, composition, and imagery to create a cohesive promotional piece.

Visual
Direction.

The initial concept and mockup establish the direction for the piece, allowing the design to be considered in context before moving into the final artwork.

Process:
Sketches.

Early sketches and visual exploration helped establish the composition before the final poster designs were developed digitally. These studies provided a space to test layout ideas and consider how the information and visual elements could work together.

Final
Artwork.

The final set brings the design direction into a series of finished promotional pieces. Together, the variations show how the visual approach can be carried across related layouts while maintaining a recognizable identity.

Reflection.

This project strengthened my approach to print-based communication, from exploring an initial direction to presenting finished promotional artwork. It reinforced the importance of balancing visual interest with clear information and considering how a design functions both as an individual piece and as part of a wider production identity.
