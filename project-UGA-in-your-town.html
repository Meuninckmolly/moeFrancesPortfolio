Overview.

UGA in Your Town is a summer traveling exhibition project that brings the University of Georgia's presence into communities beyond campus. My work supports the exhibition through a range of visual materials created for both physical and digital audiences.

My contributions include exhibition signage, bifold advertising materials, social media posts, and website assets. Across these formats, the goal is to communicate information clearly while helping maintain a cohesive visual presence for the project.
