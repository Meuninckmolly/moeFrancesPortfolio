Overview.

This interactive website presents an entrepreneurial case study on Neil Ratanji, exploring his origin story, business model, resources and tradeoffs, risk management, challenges, and founding team dynamics. Through interactive timelines, clickable diagrams, and expandable sections, the site organizes key information into an engaging digital experience that examines both the founder and the development of his venture.
